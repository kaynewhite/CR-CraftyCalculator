## 1. Getting Started (Users)

Crafty Rachel is a web-based pricing and inventory tool built for crafters, artisans, and small business owners. It helps you calculate accurate product prices, track raw materials, and save your work for future reference.

You can access Crafty Rachel from any browser on your phone, tablet, or computer — no installation required.

**Website:** Open your browser and navigate to the Crafty Rachel URL provided to you.

---

## 2. Signing Up

1. On the landing page, click **Get Started** or **Start for Free**.
2. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and a **Password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. Click **Create Account**.
4. Check your email inbox for a **6-digit verification code** (OTP). It expires in 10 minutes.
5. Enter the code on the verification screen and click **Verify**.
6. You are now logged in and will land on your personal Dashboard.

> **Tip:** If you did not receive the code, click **Resend Code**. You may request up to 3 codes per hour.

---

## 3. Logging In

1. Click **Sign In** on the landing page.
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

If you forgot your password:
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen.
2. Enter your email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the reset link sent to your email (valid for 24 hours).
4. Enter and confirm your new password.

---

## 4. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen. It shows:

- **Summary cards** — total calculations run, total materials tracked, and recent profit averages.
- **Recent Saved Calculations** — your three most recently saved pricing calculations.
- **Quick links** — shortcuts to the Calculator, Inventory, and Subscription pages.
- **Subscription expiry banner** — if your paid plan is expiring within 7 days (or has already expired), a banner will appear at the top.

Use the **sidebar** on the left to navigate between pages. On mobile, tap the hamburger (☰) icon to open the sidebar.

## 5. Pricing Calculator

The Calculator is the core tool of Crafty Rachel. It computes the selling price for a product based on your materials, labor, waste, and desired profit.

### How to use the Calculator

1. Navigate to **Calculator** in the sidebar.
2. Fill in the **Product Name**.
3. Select a **product type / category** (e.g., Business Cards, Flyers, General Printing).
4. Set the **batch quantity** — how many units you are producing in one batch.
5. Add **materials**:
   - Click **Add Material** or pull from your Inventory.
   - For each material, enter the name, quantity used, cost per unit, and unit type.
   - Optional: set a **waste percentage** (0–100%) to account for spoilage.
6. Add **printing cost** (total for the batch) and **labor cost** (total for the batch).
7. Set your desired **markup percentage** — the profit percentage on top of the total cost.
8. The calculator will instantly show:
   - **Total Material Cost** (with waste applied)
   - **Total Cost** (materials + printing + labor)
   - **Selling Price** = Total Cost × (1 + Markup ÷ 100)
   - **Profit per batch** and **profit per unit**

### Saving a Calculation

- Click **Save Calculation** to store the result.
- Saved calculations appear in the **History** page.
- Free plan users can save up to **3 calculations** (they expire after 30 days).
- Basic plan users can save up to **10 calculations** (they expire after 60 days).
- Pro plan users have **unlimited saves**.

### Refreshing Material Prices

- Click the **Refresh** button (↻) at the top-right of the Calculator page to reload the latest material prices from your Inventory.

## 6. Material Inventory

The Inventory is where you store your raw materials and their costs. The Calculator draws from this list automatically.

### Adding a Material

1. Go to **Inventory** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Material**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — must be unique (e.g., "A4 Bond Paper").
   - **Quantity** — how many units you currently have.
   - **Cost per Unit** — the price of one unit.
   - **Unit** — piece, pack, roll, sheet, ream, or meter.
   - **Category** — optional grouping (e.g., Paper, Adhesive).
4. Click **Save**.

### Editing & Deleting Materials

- Click the **edit (pencil)** icon next to any material to update it.
- Click the **delete (trash)** icon to remove it. Deletion is permanent.

### Inventory Limits by Plan

| Plan  | Max Materials |
|-------|--------------|
| Free  | 10           |
| Basic | 50           |
| Pro   | Unlimited    |

## 7. Saved Calculations (History)

The **History** page shows all calculations you have saved.

- **Search** by product name.
- **Sort** by date, profit, or name.
- Click any calculation to **view its full breakdown** (materials, costs, profit).
- Click the **trash** icon to delete a calculation permanently.

> Saved calculations expire based on your plan (30 days for Free, 60 days for Basic). Pro plan saves never expire.

## 9. Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Subscription** in the sidebar.
2. Select the plan you want (**Basic** or **Pro**).
3. Click **Select Plan**.
4. A payment modal appears — choose your payment method (**GCash** or **PayMaya**).
5. Scan the QR code shown with your e-wallet app and complete the payment.
6. Upload a **screenshot** of your payment confirmation.
7. Click **Submit**.

Your request is sent to an admin for review. Once approved, your plan is upgraded and the new limits apply immediately.

> **Note:** You can only have one pending payment request at a time. Wait for the current request to be approved or rejected before submitting another.

## 10. Your Profile

Go to **Profile** in the sidebar to:

- **View** your name, email, and current plan.
- **Edit** your name or email — click **Edit**, make changes, then click **Save Changes**.
- **Change your password** — enter your current password, then your new password (minimum 8 characters), and click **Update Password**.
- **Reset via Email** — if you cannot remember your current password, click **Reset via Email** to receive a password reset link.
- **Refresh** — click the ↻ Refresh button to reload your profile from the server.

---

## 11. Notifications

The **bell icon** (🔔) in the top-right corner of each page shows your notifications.

Notifications inform you about:
- Payment request **approved** (your plan has been upgraded).
- Payment request **rejected** (with a reason, if provided by the admin).

Click a notification to mark it as read. Unread notifications show a badge count on the bell icon.

## 12. Submitting a Report (Users)

If you encounter a bug, a problem, or want to share feedback, you can submit a report directly to the admin team.

1. Go to **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Select a **report type**:
   - **Bug** — something in the app is broken or behaving unexpectedly.
   - **Problem** — something is not working the way you expected.
   - **Feedback** — a suggestion or general comment.
   - **Other** — anything that doesn't fit the above.
3. Enter a short **Subject** (what the issue is about).
4. Write a detailed **Description** — the more detail you provide, the faster the issue can be resolved.
5. Click **Submit Report**.

You can track all your submitted reports under the **My Reports** tab on the same page. Each report shows its current status:

| Status   | Meaning                                                       |
|----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Open     | Submitted and waiting for admin review                        |
| Seen     | The admin has opened and read your report                     |
| Resolved | The admin has addressed the issue                             |
| Closed   | The report has been closed                                    |

If the admin replies to your report, the reply will appear directly on your report card.

## 13. Admin Guide

Admins have access to all regular user features (Calculator, Inventory, History, Profile, Subscription) in addition to the admin control panel. This means admins can use the platform as a regular user while also managing the platform.

To access the admin panel, simply log in with your admin credentials. You will be taken directly to the Admin Dashboard.

> Admins navigate between admin tools and regular user features using the same sidebar.

---

## 14. Admin: Managing Users

Go to **User Management** in the admin sidebar.

The user list shows all registered users with their name, email, plan, status, and last seen time.

### Actions available per user

| Action          | Description                                                  |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Suspend**     | Blocks the user from logging in. Reversible.                 |
| **Reactivate**  | Restores access for a suspended user.                        |
| **View Details**| Opens a side panel showing the user's full profile, plan, and subscription history. |

### Searching & Filtering

- Use the **search bar** to find users by name or email.
- Use the **filter dropdown** to show only Active, Suspended, or all users.

---

## 15. Admin: Approving Payments

Go to **Payment Approvals** in the admin sidebar.

Each row shows the user's name, the plan they requested, the payment method, the screenshot they uploaded, and when they submitted.

### To approve a payment

1. Click **Approve** next to the request.
2. The user's subscription is upgraded immediately.
3. A notification is sent to the user.

### To reject a payment

1. Click **Reject**.
2. Optionally enter a reason (the user will see this).
3. Click **Confirm Reject**.
4. A notification is sent to the user.

> Approved or rejected requests are removed from the pending list and moved to history.

---

## 16. Admin: Managing Payment QR Codes

Go to **QR Codes** in the admin sidebar.

This is where you upload the GCash and PayMaya QR codes that users scan to pay for subscriptions.

### Uploading a QR code

1. Select the method (Maya or GCash) from the dropdown.
2. Click **Choose File** and select an image of your QR code.
3. A preview appears — confirm it is correct.
4. Click **Save QR Code**.

### Removing a QR code

Click **Remove** under the current QR image. Users will see a notice that no QR is set up for that method.

> **Important:** Keep your QR codes up to date. If no QR is uploaded, users cannot complete payment by scanning.

---

## 17. Admin: Dashboard & Stats

The **Admin Dashboard** shows a platform-wide overview:

- **Total Users** — all registered accounts.
- **Active Subscriptions** — users currently on a paid plan.
- **Pending Payments** — payment requests awaiting review.
- **Total Revenue** — cumulative approved payments.
- **Revenue Chart** — a bar chart of monthly revenue trends.
- **Recent Payment Requests** — the latest pending items for quick action.

Click **Refresh** to reload the stats without leaving the page.

---

## 18. Admin: Reports

Go to **Reports** in the admin sidebar to manage reports submitted by users, and to submit your own reports to the Superadmin.

### Viewing User Reports

The **User Reports** tab lists all reports submitted by regular users.

Each report shows the reporter's name, email, report type, status, and subject. Click a report to expand it and see:
- The full description
- Any reply you have already sent
- Action buttons

### Replying to a User Report

1. Expand the report by clicking on it.
2. Type your response in the **Reply** text area.
3. Click **Send Reply**.

The user will see your reply on their Reports page.

### Forwarding to Superadmin

If a user report requires escalation:
1. Expand the report.
2. Click **Forward to Superadmin**.
3. The report is immediately visible to the Superadmin.

> Once forwarded, a report cannot be unforwarded.

### Resolving a Report

Click **Resolve** to mark a report as resolved. The user's report card will update to show **Resolved** status.

### Submitting Your Own Report

Use the **Submit to Superadmin** tab to send a report directly to the Superadmin. This is for platform-level issues, bugs, or suggestions that require Superadmin attention.

1. Select the report type.
2. Enter a subject and description.
3. Click **Submit to Superadmin**.
